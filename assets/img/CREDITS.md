# Image credits

All photography on this page comes from Wikimedia Commons under the licences
listed below. Files were downsized for web use; no other alterations were made.

**None of these photographs depict Rob Wigboldus Vishandel, its staff, or its
food**, with the single exception of `alley-zoutsteeg.jpg`, which shows the
public street the shop stands on. They illustrate the subject matter — Dutch
herring, the fish counter tradition, the alley — and are never captioned or
framed as pictures of this business.
