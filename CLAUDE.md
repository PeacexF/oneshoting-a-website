# Workflow

Build a polished landing page for the business specified by the user.

1. **Research first** — use publicly available/open-source information to understand the business, its products/services, location, audience, and existing online presence.
2. **Verify facts** — don't invent business details, claims, prices, testimonials, or history. If something can't be verified, leave it out or keep it generic.
3. **Design from the research** — create a visual identity and page structure that actually fits the business rather than using a generic template.
4. **Implement completely** — build the full responsive landing page with real content, imagery where appropriate, working links/CTAs, and good mobile/desktop UX.
5. **Polish** — review the result as if it were being delivered to the business owner. Fix awkward copy, spacing, responsiveness, broken links, and anything that looks obviously AI-generated.

Do not ask the user for design decisions. Make reasonable decisions yourself and ship the best version you can.
